
# M4 Track — Day 27: Closed-Loop Optimization & Model Retraining Architecture

## 1. System Overview

The SupplyPrescript M4 engine implements an end-to-end continuous learning and optimization feedback loop. Prescriptions generated by the SciPy HiGHS solver are audited against real-world delivery realizations captured by the Application Team's platform, enabling automated drift detection and controlled model retraining.

---

## 2. End-to-End Operational Lifecycle

1. **ML Inference & Optimization (`models/m4_pipeline_bridge.py`)**:

   * Raw shipment attributes are evaluated by the XGBoost delay risk classifier.
   * Projected delay days and order values are mapped into the canonical LP objective matrix.
   * HiGHS solver assigns optimal mitigation routes (`OPT-A`, `OPT-B`, or `OPT-C`) honoring capacity ($\le 20\%$ A, $\le 35\%$ B) and budget caps ($\le \$399.98$).
2. **Telemetry Ingestion & Schema Audit (`models/m4_validate_writeback_payload.py`)**:

   * Upon shipment arrival, actual delivery durations and incurred penalties write back to the telemetry interface.
   * Payloads undergo validation enforcing schema types, non-negativity, and option ID validity.
3. **Drift & Discrepancy Evaluation (`models/m4_threshold_checker.py`)**:

   * Telemetry batches are evaluated against rolling operational thresholds:
     * **Discrepancy Rate**: $|D_{\text{actual}} - D_{\text{predicted}}| \ge 2\text{ days}$ must remain below $15\%$.
     * **Severe False Negatives**: Realized delays $\ge 3\text{ days}$ with predicted $\le 1\text{ day}$ must remain below $5\%$.
     * **Severe Disruption Spike**: Extreme drift ($\ge 4\text{ days}$) must remain below $2\%$.
     * **Financial Drift**: Realized SLA penalties must not exceed projected baseline penalties by $\ge 20\%$.
   * Batches under $N < 10$ records are held in evaluation buffers to prevent sample-size noise.
4. **Retraining Trigger Orchestration (`models/m4_retrain_trigger_skeleton.py`, `models/m4_retrain_trigger_runner.py`)**:

   * Tripped thresholds initiate the candidate re-fitting pipeline.
   * Candidate models are trained on accumulated historical telemetry plus newly audited records.
5. **Promotion Gates & Hot-Swapping**:

   * Candidate models must meet ROC-AUC retention ($\Delta \text{AUC} \ge -1.0\%$) and latency constraints ($< 50\text{ ms}$ for $N=100$) before replacing `models/improved_xgboost.json`.
   * Downstream solver regression suites are executed before final deployment.
